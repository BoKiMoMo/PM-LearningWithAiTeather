# 📘 DAY 15 – 產品策略與 Roadmap 規劃
(DAY 15 – Product Strategy & Roadmap Planning)

---

## 🎯 主題說明｜Topic Introduction

**中文：**
產品策略（Product Strategy）是產品長期方向與價值定位的核心，而 Roadmap（產品路線圖）則是將策略轉換為可執行的階段計劃。今天的課程將教你如何制定產品策略，並繪製對應的 Roadmap，讓團隊與目標保持一致。

**English:**
Product Strategy defines the long-term direction and value positioning of a product, while a Roadmap translates that strategy into actionable, phased plans. Today’s lesson covers how to craft a product strategy and create a corresponding roadmap to keep the team aligned with objectives.

---

## 🧠 概念導入｜Concept Analogy

**中文：**
產品策略就像導航的目的地，Roadmap 就是地圖上的路線規劃，告訴你如何一步步抵達。

**English:**
Product Strategy is like the destination in your GPS, and the Roadmap is the planned route showing how you’ll get there step-by-step.

---

## 🔍 制定產品策略的三步驟｜3 Steps to Build a Product Strategy

1. **市場與用戶洞察（Market & User Insights）**
   - 中文：分析市場規模、競爭格局與目標用戶痛點。
   - English: Analyze market size, competitive landscape, and target user pain points.

2. **產品定位（Product Positioning）**
   - 中文：明確你的產品價值主張與差異化優勢。
   - English: Define your product’s value proposition and differentiation.

3. **策略目標（Strategic Goals）**
   - 中文：設定 6–12 個月內的主要目標（如營收、用戶數、留存率）。
   - English: Set key goals for the next 6–12 months (e.g., revenue, user growth, retention).

---

## 📅 Roadmap 四大類型｜4 Types of Roadmaps

1. **功能型（Feature-based）**
   - 中文：按功能發布時間安排。
   - English: Organized by feature release timeline.

2. **目標型（Goal-based）**
   - 中文：依照策略目標來安排。
   - English: Organized by strategic goals.

3. **主題型（Theme-based）**
   - 中文：以核心主題（如「提升用戶留存」）劃分。
   - English: Organized by themes (e.g., “Improve User Retention”).

4. **混合型（Hybrid）**
   - 中文：結合上述多種方式。
   - English: A combination of the above approaches.

## 🎯 小結 Summary

**中文：**
產品策略是方向，Roadmap 是行動藍圖。兩者結合才能確保團隊不會迷失方向，並能在時間內達成目標。

**English:**
Product Strategy sets the direction, and the Roadmap is the action blueprint. Together, they ensure the team stays on track and achieves goals on time.
