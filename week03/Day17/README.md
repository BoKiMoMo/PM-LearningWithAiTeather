# 📘 DAY 17 – 建立 Notion 作品集頁面
(DAY 17 – Building a Notion Portfolio Page)

---

## 🎯 主題說明｜Topic Introduction

**中文：**
今天的目標是將你過去幾週完成的產品經理與 C++ 學習成果，整理到 Notion 作品集頁面中，方便日後面試、展示與自我回顧。

**English:**
Today’s goal is to organize your PM and C++ learning deliverables from the past weeks into a Notion portfolio page, making it easy to showcase in interviews, presentations, or for self-review.

---

## 🧠 概念導入｜Concept Analogy

**中文：**
可以把 Notion 作品集想成你的「線上履歷＋專案展覽館」，它不僅放你的專案成果，也記錄你的思考過程與成長軌跡。

**English:**
Think of a Notion portfolio as your “online resume + project gallery,” not only storing your project deliverables but also documenting your thought process and growth journey.

---

## 🔍 建立步驟｜Building Steps

1. **建立主頁面**
   - 中文：在 Notion 中建立一個新頁面，命名為「[你的名字] – Portfolio」。
   - English: Create a new page in Notion titled “[Your Name] – Portfolio”.

2. **設計目錄結構**
   - 中文：將作品集分成不同類別，如「產品經理專案」、「C++ 專案」、「技術文件」、「學習紀錄」。
   - English: Divide the portfolio into categories such as “PM Projects,” “C++ Projects,” “Technical Docs,” and “Learning Records.”

3. **上傳與整理內容**
   - 中文：將 PRD、Wireframe、MVP、簡報、程式碼連結等放入對應分類。
   - English: Upload and organize PRD, Wireframe, MVP, presentations, and code links into relevant categories.

4. **加上摘要與圖片**
   - 中文：每個專案加上 3–5 句的摘要，並附上 1–2 張圖片或截圖。
   - English: Add a 3–5 sentence summary and include 1–2 images or screenshots for each project.

5. **優化閱讀體驗**
   - 中文：使用 Notion 區塊功能（Toggle、Callout、Gallery View）增加可讀性。
   - English: Use Notion blocks (Toggle, Callout, Gallery View) to improve readability.

---

## ✍ 實作任務｜Practice

1. **中文：** 在 Notion 建立作品集首頁，完成至少 3 個專案區塊。
2. **English:** Create a portfolio homepage in Notion with at least 3 project sections.
