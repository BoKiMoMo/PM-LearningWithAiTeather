# 📘 DAY 13 – 產品簡報製作（內部提案）
(DAY 13 – Product Presentation for Internal Proposal)

---

## 🎯 主題說明｜Topic Introduction

**中文：**
產品簡報是 PM 與團隊溝通產品願景、功能與計劃的重要工具。今天將學習如何製作一份內部提案簡報，讓團隊快速理解產品價值與實施步驟。

**English:**
A product presentation is an essential tool for PMs to communicate product vision, features, and plans with the team. Today we’ll learn how to create an internal proposal that helps the team quickly understand the product value and execution plan.

---

## 🧠 概念導入｜Concept Analogy

**中文：**
製作產品簡報就像帶領團隊看地圖，你要清楚指出目的地（產品願景）、路線（開發計劃）與沿途的關鍵地標（功能與里程碑）。

**English:**
Creating a product presentation is like guiding your team with a map. You need to clearly show the destination (product vision), the route (development plan), and the key landmarks along the way (features and milestones).

---

## 🔍 核心簡報架構｜Core Presentation Structure

1. **產品願景（Product Vision）**
   - 中文：一句話描述產品存在的核心價值。  
   - English: One sentence describing the core value of the product.

2. **目標用戶（Target Audience）**
   - 中文：明確定義誰會使用這個產品。  
   - English: Clearly define who will use the product.

3. **核心功能（Core Features）**
   - 中文：列出 3~5 個主要功能，並簡述其價值。  
   - English: List 3–5 main features and explain their value.

4. **市場與競品分析（Market & Competitor Analysis）**
   - 中文：指出市場機會與差異化優勢。  
   - English: Highlight market opportunities and differentiation.

5. **開發計劃與時程（Development Plan & Timeline）**
   - 中文：用 Roadmap 呈現主要開發階段與里程碑。  
   - English: Use a roadmap to show development stages and milestones.

6. **KPI 與衡量方式（KPIs & Metrics）**
   - 中文：設定成功衡量指標（如留存率、轉換率）。  
   - English: Define success metrics (e.g., retention rate, conversion rate).

## 🔊 技術英文句型｜Tech English Practice

- "Our product aims to address the gap in **[market need]**."
- "This feature will help users **[achieve specific benefit]**."
- "Our development plan includes **[milestone]** by **[date]**."
